USAGE
  Use -dark files on dark backgrounds, -light on white/light.
  Use -transparent when placing over a photo or colored panel.
  PNGs are rendered at 4x for retina and print.
  Minimum mark size: 24px. Below that use the favicon file.
  Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the "S".

MEANING
  The S is formed by two opposing arcs: the upper arc gathers many
  somatic cells inward (reprogramming), the lower arc releases one
  pluripotent state outward into many fates (differentiation).
  The nucleated cell at the waist is the iPSC. The colony at the
  entry is the starting population; the nucleated cells at the exit
  are differentiated therapeutic products.
